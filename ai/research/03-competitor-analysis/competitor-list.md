# Competitor List

Identified via Google SERP results for "spray foam rig maintenance", "off ratio spray foam",
and "spray foam proportioner" (DataForSEO SERP, 2026-08-20). All are real, established
equipment/distribution companies — content-competitor analysis only, not scraped verbatim, no
endorsement implied.

1. **IDI Distributors** — idi-insulation.com/spf-repair — wholesale spray foam distributor,
   ranks for rig maintenance/repair support content.
2. **Service Partners** — service-partners.com/spray-foam-rig-repair — distributor, rig repair
   service page.
3. **AMD Distribution** — amddistribution.com/blog/tips-for-maintaining-spray-foam-rig —
   distributor blog content on rig maintenance.
4. **Graco Inc.** — graco.com — major equipment manufacturer (proportioners/reactors), strong
   technical support/education-center content.
5. **Accufoam** — accufoam.com/post/identify-and-prevent-off-ratio-foam — off-ratio
   troubleshooting blog content.
6. **Polymac USA** — polymac-usa.com/spray-foam-proportioners — proportioner manufacturer,
   product-focused.
7. **Profoam Corporation** — profoam.com — rig/proportioner seller with a service center.

Note: these are all reviewed at the SERP-snippet/page-title level, not via full page crawls —
flagged as such per the same discipline used in the prior sprayfoam3d.com build's competitor
research.
