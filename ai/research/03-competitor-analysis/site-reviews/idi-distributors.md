# Competitor Review: IDI Distributors

**URL:** idi-insulation.com/spf-repair, idi-insulation.com/spf-tech-support
**Google ranking position:** #1-2 for "spray foam rig maintenance"

## Observed (from SERP snippet/page title — not full crawl)
- Positions itself as a wholesale distributor with attached rig tech support: on-site
  inspection, training, phone support, on-site repairs, full rig rebuilds.
- Content is service-and-support-first, tied directly to being a distributor (i.e. the content
  exists to support customers of their equipment sales, not as independent education).

## Strengths
1. Real depth of service offering (phone support → on-site repair → full rebuild) signals real
   expertise.
2. Ranks well for the exact maintenance/repair query set.

## Weaknesses / Gap
1. Content is vendor-tied — a contractor reading it is implicitly being sold IDI's
   distribution/support relationship, not getting vendor-neutral education.
2. No indication of consumer/homeowner-facing content explaining why this matters for job
   quality — purely B2B/contractor-support framed.

## Specific Thing to Learn From
Their explicit tiering of support (phone → on-site → full rebuild) is a useful structural idea
for explaining "what maintenance/troubleshooting looks like in practice" without copying their
specific offering.
