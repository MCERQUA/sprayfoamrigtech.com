# Competitor Review: Graco Inc.

**URL:** graco.com/us/en/contractor/support/education-center/reactor-support/reactor-maintenance-repair.html
**Google ranking position:** top-5 for "spray foam rig maintenance"

## Observed (from SERP snippet/page title — not full crawl)
- Major equipment manufacturer (makes the Reactor proportioner line widely used across the
  industry) with a dedicated "Education Center" — the most substantial, manufacturer-authority
  content in this competitive set.

## Strengths
1. Real manufacturer authority — they build the equipment, so their maintenance guidance carries
   real weight.
2. "Education Center" framing signals genuine investment in educational content, not just sales.

## Weaknesses / Gap
1. Product-specific (Reactor-line only) — doesn't cover the category broadly (other
   manufacturers' proportioners, general rig concepts).
2. No apparent consumer/job-quality connective content.

## Specific Thing to Learn From
The "Education Center" framing (dedicated, named educational content hub separate from sales
pages) is a strong structural precedent for how a credible equipment-education resource should
present itself — worth matching in tone/seriousness, not in specific product content.
