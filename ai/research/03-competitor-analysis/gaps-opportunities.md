# Gaps & Opportunities

## Things NO competitor does well
1. **Vendor-neutral, cross-topic education** — every competitor's content is tied to their own
   equipment/distribution sales funnel. OPPORTUNITY: build genuinely independent education (what
   a rig is → maintenance/troubleshooting → why it matters) with zero vendor endorsement,
   funneling to CCA's insured-contractor network instead of an equipment sale.
2. **Connecting equipment quality to job outcomes for a non-technical reader** — all competitor
   content assumes a contractor/technical reader. OPPORTUNITY: our "Why Rig Quality Matters"
   pillar serves BOTH the technical contractor audience AND a homeowner/property-owner audience
   evaluating a contractor, which no competitor attempts.

## Things only 1-2 competitors do
1. Graco's "Education Center" framing (dedicated, named educational hub) — we should: adopt
   similarly serious, dedicated framing rather than a thin blog-tips approach.

## Content gaps
1. Nobody publishes an honest "off-ratio foam causes real job problems, here's what to look for
   and what it means for you" piece aimed at BOTH contractors diagnosing their own rig AND
   homeowners trying to understand why a job might have gone wrong — we can own this.
2. Nobody connects rig maintenance/troubleshooting to the "why does spray foam sometimes get
   removed / flagged at resale" question — the honest answer (per PAA research) usually traces
   back to improper application, which traces back to off-ratio foam, which traces back to
   equipment/maintenance issues. This causal chain is a genuine content gap we can own honestly.

## Our differentiation strategy
1. Genuinely vendor-neutral (no equipment sales, no distributor tie-in) — pure education +
   contractor-matching funnel.
2. Cross-topic authority: equipment → maintenance/troubleshooting → job-quality-and-outcomes, as
   one coherent, cross-linked resource instead of three isolated pieces.
3. Dual-audience framing (contractor + homeowner) that no single competitor attempts.
