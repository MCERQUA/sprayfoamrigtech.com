# Competitor Pattern Analysis

## Common Pattern Across All 5 Reviewed
Every competitor's rig-maintenance/troubleshooting content is **vendor-tied** — a distributor's
service page, a manufacturer's product-specific education center, or a single blog post — none
is an independent, cross-topic, vendor-neutral resource.

## Common Content Shape
- Distributor sites (IDI, Service Partners, AMD): service-page or blog "tips" format, tied to
  selling/supporting their own equipment or distribution relationship.
- Manufacturer sites (Graco, Polymac): product-specific technical support, framed as an
  "Education Center" (Graco) — the strongest single-source authority, but narrow (their product
  line only).
- Single-topic blog posts (Accufoam): good topical depth on one narrow question (off-ratio foam)
  but no broader resource around it.

## Trust Signal Patterns
- Manufacturer/distributor authority (being the company that makes or sells the equipment) is
  the dominant trust signal in this niche — nobody relies on testimonials or review counts for
  this specific content category.

## Design Trends in This Niche
- Product/service-page-first design (not editorial/magazine style) — utilitarian, spec-sheet
  adjacent. This is a genuinely under-designed content niche compared to more consumer-facing
  insulation topics.

## Content Patterns
- No competitor connects "what the equipment is" + "how to maintain/troubleshoot it" + "why it
  matters for the finished job" into one coherent, cross-linked resource — each piece of content
  lives in isolation, tied to its own vendor's sales funnel.
