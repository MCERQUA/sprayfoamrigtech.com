# Conversion Funnel

## How the dual audience arrives and converts
1. **Awareness:** A contractor has a rig problem (off-ratio foam, equipment failure) and
   searches for a fix; OR a homeowner encounters "spray foam removed/flagged" content and wants
   to understand why.
2. **Research:** Contractor reads maintenance/troubleshooting content to diagnose their issue;
   homeowner reads the "why it matters" pillar to understand the causal chain.
3. **Evaluation:** Both audiences arrive at the same conclusion — equipment quality and
   maintenance directly affect job outcomes — which is where the CCA network angle becomes
   relevant (a well-equipped, maintained-rig contractor is a safer hire).
4. **Decision:** Trust built through genuinely useful, vendor-neutral technical content (not
   hype, not sales pressure).
5. **Action:** Get Matched lead form or phone call.

## Page elements per stage
- Stage 1-2: Hero + pillar-page technical depth (this IS the content, not just a funnel step)
- Stage 3: Cross-linked pillar pages building the full causal picture
- Stage 4: "Why Rig Quality Matters" honesty framing + explicit vendor-neutrality
- Stage 5: Repeated Get Matched CTA + phone number
