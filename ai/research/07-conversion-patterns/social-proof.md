# Social Proof

No fabricated testimonials, review counts, or "as seen in" claims anywhere on this site — hard
rule, zero tolerance. This niche's competitor set doesn't rely on social proof either (it's a
technical-authority niche, not a review-driven one), so there's no competitive pressure to
fabricate any. Real, verifiable trust signals only: canonical CCA contact info, transparent
"who runs this and why" positioning, and explicit vendor-neutrality.
