# Trust Signals

## Industry-Specific Trust
- Manufacturer/distributor authority is the dominant trust signal among competitors — we
  substitute this with genuine vendor-neutrality + CCA's real insurance-industry positioning
  (an agency that works with spray foam contractors, not a product seller).

## Social Proof
- No fabricated testimonials or review counts (hard rule — zero tolerance, applies here as
  everywhere in this batch).
- No competitor in this niche relies on testimonials either — genuinely a technical-authority
  niche, not a social-proof-driven one.

## Authority Signals
- CCA's real positioning: an insurance agency that works with spray foam contractors daily —
  same authority framing used on roofmetaverse.com and sprayfoam3d.com.
- Vendor-neutrality itself is a trust signal in this specific niche (see gaps-opportunities.md —
  nobody else in this space is vendor-neutral).

## Our Trust Signal Plan
**Must have:**
1. Canonical contact info visible in header/footer
2. Clear "who runs this and why" (About page)
3. Explicit vendor-neutrality statement (we don't sell or endorse equipment)

**Explicitly excluded:**
1. Fabricated testimonials, review counts, or customer counts
2. Any implied endorsement of a specific manufacturer/distributor
