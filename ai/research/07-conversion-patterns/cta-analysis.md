# CTA Analysis

| Competitor | CTA Text | Placement | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDI Distributors | "Call for support" / repair request | Service page | Text link, weak |
| Service Partners | "Request repair" | Service page | Button, low prominence |
| Graco | "Contact support" | Education Center | Text link |

## Most Effective Patterns
- None of the competitors use a persistent, repeated CTA — this niche's content is genuinely
  weak on conversion design (utilitarian service pages, not conversion-engineered).
- Opportunity: apply the same repeated-CTA-every-2-3-sections discipline used across the rest of
  the fleet's builds (roofmetaverse.com, sprayfoam3d.com) — this niche has essentially no
  competitive pressure on CTA design.

## Our CTA Strategy
- **Primary CTA: "Get Matched with a Well-Equipped, Insured Spray Foam Contractor"** — hero +
  repeated every 2-3 sections + final CTA band, same pattern as sprayfoam3d.com.
- **Secondary CTA: "See How It Works"** — anchors into the pillar content from the homepage hero.
- Phone CTA: 844-967-5247 visible in header/footer.
