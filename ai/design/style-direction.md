# Style Direction — SprayFoamRigTech

## Reference anchor
A shop manual / trade-publication feel — mechanical, plain-spoken, equipment-literate. Not a
sales page for any vendor, not editorial thought-leadership like roofmetaverse.com/
sprayfoam3d.com (though structurally similar), and explicitly NOT leaning into any "tech" hype
despite the domain name — this is about proportioners and hoses, not abstraction.

Primary hue (23°) sits between roofmetaverse.com's copper (21°) and venardsprayfoam.com's
goldenrod (33°), but the pine-green trust accent (107°) is genuinely distinct from every other
green used tonight (sage 89°, verdigris 160°, marsh 76°, olive-moss 75°) — combined with a
different type pairing and a literal-equipment motif (not an abstract "scan/measure" device),
the system reads as its own identity.

## Typography
- **Display/headline:** `Oswald` — a condensed, utilitarian, sign-painter/industrial-stencil-
  adjacent sans, distinct from every other headline face used tonight (serif/slab/geometric) —
  reads like equipment labeling, fitting for a rig-equipment site.
- **Body/UI:** `Inter` — reused as a body workhorse (already proven reliable, not a
  differentiator field) but paired with Oswald for a genuinely different overall feel.

## Visual motif
**"Dialed In."** A literal pressure-gauge/dial device — a simple gauge-needle icon used for
trust badges and stat callouts, plus a coiled-hose line motif as a subtle section-divider
texture. This is deliberately literal and mechanical, not abstract — fitting the mundane,
bought-equipment nature of the niche (the opposite instinct from the "measured, not guessed"
abstraction used on sprayfoam3d.com).

## Motion language
Standard scroll-reveal, hover-lift cards, pine-checkmark draw-in, sticky CTA — same base
patterns as prior builds, applied to this palette.

## Imagery direction
Real-context photography: a spray foam rig/trailer exterior, a proportioner control panel
close-up, heated hoses coiled and connected, a technician servicing rig equipment. Warm
rust/khaki color grading, genuine shop/job-site lighting — NOT clean studio product photography,
NOT neon-tech overlays.

## Section rhythm plan (every page)
Rotate: parchment base → tan tinted band → charcoal dark inverted contrast band → khaki accent
wash → full-bleed photo band w/ warm overlay ≥50% opacity. No two adjacent sections share a
background. At least one charcoal dark band per page.
