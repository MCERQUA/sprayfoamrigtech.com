# Design Brief — SprayFoamRigTech (MASTER)

## What this site is
A CCA educational content hub on spray foam rig equipment — what a rig is (proportioner, spray
gun, heated hoses, generator), how to maintain and troubleshoot one, and why equipment quality
matters for job outcomes. Real, mundane, bought-equipment topic — no real-business collision
found (see `../research/01-business-profile/profile.md` for the check), no speculative-tech
framing despite the domain name.

## Palette / type / motif
See `style-direction.md` — rust-orange/khaki-gold/pine-green/charcoal system, Oswald + Inter,
"Dialed In" gauge/hose motif. Deliberately distinct from all 4 other fleet builds this batch.

## Section rhythm (every page)
Parchment base → tan tinted band → charcoal dark inverted band → khaki accent wash →
full-bleed photo band w/ ≥50%-opacity warm overlay. Never two adjacent same-background sections.

## Conversion funnel
Land (curious/educational, mostly contractor-side but homeowner-relevant) → read real
equipment/maintenance content → repeated CTA exposure ("Get Matched with a Well-Equipped,
Insured Spray Foam Contractor") → convert on lead form or phone call.

and plain-spoken throughout, per profile.md's angle-validation note.
