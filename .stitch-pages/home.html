Technical Analysis

On-Ratio vs Off-Ratio

The proportioner is the heart of the rig, responsible for mixing the A-side (ISO) and B-side (Resin) chemicals at exactly a 1:1 ratio.

On-Ratio Foam expands properly, cures instantly, and provides maximum R-value. It is structurally sound and environmentally safe.

Off-Ratio Foam occurs when pressure or temperature drops on one side. The result is a sticky, shrinking mess that lacks insulating properties and can off-gas harmful odors. Quality equipment is the only defense against off-ratio failure.
