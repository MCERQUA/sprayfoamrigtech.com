The Science of Consistency

Spray foam isn't just a material; it's a chemical reaction engineered on-site. The rig dictates the precision of that reaction.

speed

Pressure Control

Exacting 1:1 ratio mixing requires perfectly balanced pressure. Fluctuation leads to off-ratio foam, compromising structural integrity.

thermostat

Thermal Baseline

Heated hoses must maintain precise temperatures to ensure proper viscosity. Cold chemicals result in poor yield and adhesion failures.
