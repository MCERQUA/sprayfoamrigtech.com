Mastering The Machine:
Rig Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Precision engineering requires proactive care. Learn the protocols that keep your proportioners running at peak efficiency and prevent costly off-ratio downtime.

Routine Maintenance

The foundation of spray foam rig longevity. Adhere to these mechanical protocols rigorously.

1

check_circle Filter Changes

FREQUENCY: DAILY/WEEKLY

Inspect and replace Y-strainer filters on proportioner inlets to prevent debris from compromising pump seals.

2

check_circle Hose Inspection

FREQUENCY: PRE-SHIFT

Conduct physical and thermal checks of heated hoses. Look for scuffs, kinks, and verify uniform temperature distribution.
